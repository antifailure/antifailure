# added

The console asks, once, how you want to start.

Signing in for the first time landed on the environments list, which is three
empty cards explaining that something appears when the engine reports one.
Somebody who came for a check on their pull requests, somebody who came to run
an environment from a terminal, and somebody who came to point a coding agent
at this control plane all saw the same three cards, and the next step for each
of them was on a different page, or on no page at all: the hosted MCP address
was shown to operators and to nobody else.

The root now asks which of the three you are and shows that one's next step,
with the command, the address or the install link, and the permissions it
needs in one line. The answer is remembered in the browser for the
organization, the other two paths stay a click away, Not now is honoured, and
Settings shows the answer with a way to change it.
