# changed

The customer console's sidebar is black, the way the operator portal's has
always been. The two are one product served from one origin and they had two
rails, an off-white one with grey text on the customer side and a near-black
one with white text on the operator side, so a person with both open saw two
generations of the same thing. The customer rail, the mobile bar, the drawer
and the reduced header a lapsed plan shows now use the portal's tokens by
value: the same black, white on the current entry and a softer white at rest,
the same pill behind the current page, and a focus ring drawn in white so
keyboard navigation is visible on the dark ground. The content beside it stays
light. Every text pair on the rail measures above 7:1.

The first sign-in walk reads as four designed screens rather than four grey
lists. The cover pane carries each step, with a headline and a line of its
own, the first still being the sign-in screen's sentence. The three places you
can use Antifailure are large tiles, three across on a desktop, with the icon,
the title, the line, and a clear tick. Each plan is set as a figure: the name
large, its six limits as a two row grid of tabular numbers, and how the plan is
come by as quiet metadata beside the name, still with no price, because the
pricing page marks its bands illustrative and Stripe holds the real one. The
setup cards sit on a numbered rail whose numbers turn into checks, Mark done
is a check control that ticks and quiets the card, and a line above them
counts the cards done. The four benefits are four rows. The words under the
progress bars name the steps. One motion exists, a 160ms fade and rise when
the step changes, and it does not run for a person who has asked their system
for less motion.
