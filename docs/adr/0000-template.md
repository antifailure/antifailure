# ADR NNNN: Title in the imperative

- **Status:** proposed | accepted | superseded by ADR-NNNN
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders:** who agreed

## Context

What forces are at play. What is true today that makes this a decision rather
than an obvious step. Include the constraint that actually binds: a provider
limit, a security requirement, a performance budget, a licensing boundary.

## Decision

One paragraph, in the present tense, stating what we do. Not what we might do.

## Consequences

What becomes easier. What becomes harder. What we are now committed to that we
were not before. What we will have to revisit and under what trigger.

## Alternatives considered

For each: what it was, and the specific reason it lost. An alternative with no
stated reason for losing is not an alternative, it is a list item.
